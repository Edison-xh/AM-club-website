Club Background

The Animation & Multimedia Club is a student creative community where members explore ideas, practise digital skills and learn from one another.

The club welcomes students who are interested in creating, learning and collaborating through animation and multimedia.

Mission and Objectives

Create

Develop ideas and express them through creative digital work.

Learn

Build practical skills through practice and knowledge sharing.

Connect

Collaborate with students and exchange constructive feedback.
